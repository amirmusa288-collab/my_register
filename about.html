About My Register

My Register is a free digital notebook built to feel like a real paper ledger — open it, find a free page, write, and remember the page number. No folders, no forced structure, no account required.

Most note-taking apps ask you to build a whole filing system before you can jot anything down. We wanted the opposite: the simplicity of a physical register that's always sitting open on the desk, ready for the next entry.

Everything you write is stored on your own device. There's no server collecting your notes, no login wall, and no ads. It's meant to be the kind of quiet, dependable tool a real notebook is — just one that can search itself, back itself up, and never runs out of pages.

My Register is built and maintained independently. If it's useful to you, the best thing you can do is tell a friend who still carries a paper diary around.
