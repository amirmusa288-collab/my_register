How to Use My Register — A Digital Notebook That Works Like a Real One

Most note-taking apps ask you to create folders, tags, and endless nested pages until your notes are technically organized but practically impossible to find. My Register takes the opposite approach. It works the way a physical register or accounts book has worked for generations: you open it, you find a free page, you write something down, and you remember the page number. That's it. No folders, no sign-up, no cloud account — just a book that lives entirely on your own device.

Opening the Register

When you first arrive, you'll see the register closed, sitting in the middle of the screen exactly like a real notebook waiting on a desk. Tap or click anywhere on it to open it. The first thing you'll see is the Index — a list of every page you've already written something on, along with the short label you gave it, plus a grid of all 100 numbered pages so you can see at a glance which ones are still empty.

Writing By Hand or By Keyboard

Open any page and you'll find two ways to write on it. The Type mode gives you a proper keyboard text area, styled like ruled paper, where you can type in either English or Urdu — just tap the EN or اردو button at the top and the page will switch its font and writing direction automatically. You can also dictate by voice using the microphone button instead of typing, and run an English Grammar Check to catch spelling and grammar issues, with one-tap fixes for the suggestions it finds. The Hand mode turns the page into a small canvas where you can write or draw using your finger or mouse, exactly like putting a pen to paper.

Turning Pages Like a Real Book

Turning a page plays a short animation of the page physically lifting and flipping to the other side, along with a soft paper-turning sound, just like flicking through a real register. You can turn pages using the arrow buttons at the top, or simply swipe.

Labels, Multiple Tags & Dates

At the bottom of every page you can give it a short Index Label, add one or more small tags like "expense" or "contact" to group similar pages together, and optionally attach a date to the page so it behaves a little like a diary entry too. All of this shows up back on the Index page so you can find things later at a glance.

Doing the Math — With Any Currency

Every page in Type mode has a small currency calculator built in. Choose a currency, enter two amounts, pick an operation, and insert the result straight into your page as a written line.

Keeping Your Register Safe

You can lock the entire register with a PIN from Settings, or lock individual sensitive pages from within the page toolbar so they stay hidden until you unlock them. My Register also supports Dark Mode for comfortable night-time writing, adjustable font size for the typed pages, and a Statistics screen that shows how much of the register you've filled.

Your Data Stays Yours

Everything you write, draw, or calculate is saved locally inside your web browser, and nothing is uploaded automatically. The one exception is the optional Grammar Check button: if you tap it, that page's English text (and only that text, only at that moment) is sent to the free LanguageTool.org service to get spelling and grammar suggestions. You can export a JSON backup of all volumes from Settings or export individual pages as PDF files whenever you need a physical copy.
