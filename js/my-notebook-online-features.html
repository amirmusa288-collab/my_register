My Notebook, Reimagined: A Complete Tour of Every Feature in My Register

Published July 2026 · 7 min read

If you've typed "my notebook" into a search bar hoping to find somewhere simple to write things down, you've probably landed on a dozen apps that all promise the same thing and deliver a cluttered dashboard instead. My Register was built as an answer to that search — a free online notebook styled after a real numbered register, with 100 pages ready the moment you open it. This post is a full tour of what's actually inside it today.

The Cover: Opening Your Notebook

Before you reach a single page, My Register opens on a cover — a title, a subtitle, and a volume number, with a short page-turn animation when you open it. It's designed to feel like picking up an actual notebook rather than loading a web form.

The Index: Your Map of 100 Pages

Every volume holds 100 numbered pages, shown as a grid you can scan at a glance. From the index you can jump straight to any page, search across everything you've written for a specific word or line, and browse a list of pages you've tagged with a short label — so something like "Ali's number" or "July rent" is findable by name instead of by hunting page by page.

Writing: Type, Handwrite, or Switch Languages Mid-Page

Inside a page, there are two distinct ways to write. Type mode works like a normal text editor, supporting both English and Urdu — Urdu renders in proper right-to-left Nastaliq script rather than a generic font swap. Hand mode turns the page into a canvas for writing with a finger, stylus, or mouse, the way you'd write in a paper notebook. Undo and redo work in both modes.

Tools Built Into the Page

  • Bookmarks — mark any page to find it again quickly
  • Version history — earlier versions of a page are kept, so an accidental overwrite isn't permanent
  • A multi-currency calculator — for tracking money or rates without switching to another app

Getting Your Pages Back Out

Every page — and the entire 100-page volume — can be exported as a PDF at any time, so your notebook is never trapped inside one browser.

What's New in My Register

A few features have been added recently to round out the "my notebook" experience:

🌙 Dark Mode

A single tap switches the whole register into a dark color palette — easier on the eyes for late-night writing. Your choice is remembered for next time, and it matches your device's own dark mode setting automatically on your first visit.

🍪 Clearer Cookie Preferences

A straightforward, one-time notice now explains the basic analytics and ad cookies used on the site, with a clear Accept or Decline choice.

💾 Backup and Restore

Since everything in My Register lives locally in your browser, there was previously no way to move your notebook to a new device or recover it if browser data was cleared. Now, right from the index screen, you can export your entire register to a single backup file and import it back — on the same device or a new one — whenever you need it.

Why It's Built This Way

Every piece of My Register comes back to one goal: making a "my notebook" experience that behaves like the physical notebook it's modeled after, minus what was always inconvenient about paper — not being searchable, not backed up, and not always legible a year later.
