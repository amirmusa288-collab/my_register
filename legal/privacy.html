Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your device. My Register does not require sign-up and does not upload your notes to any server by default. Everything you write, draw, or calculate is saved locally in your browser's storage.

Grammar Check: if you tap the ✅ Grammar button, that page's text — and only that text, only at that moment — is sent to the free LanguageTool.org service to check spelling and grammar. No other page is sent.

Live currency rates: tapping 🌐 Live Rates fetches current exchange rates from a public rates API. No personal data or note content is sent — only the request for rates.

Backups: when you export a JSON backup or a PDF, that file is generated and downloaded directly to your device. It does not pass through our servers.

Photos and drawings: images you attach and hand-drawn pages are stored the same way as typed text — locally, on your device.

We don't use tracking cookies, analytics, or sell data, because we don't collect any to begin with. If this policy ever changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
